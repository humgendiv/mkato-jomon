Jomon Archaeological Sites Information

   Period: Initial Jomon (5,000-4,000 BP)

   Coordinates: 32.69°N, 130.66°E
   Period: Early Jomon (7,000-5,500 BP)

3. Mitsusawa (三ツ沢遺跡)
   Location: Yokohama, Kanagawa
   Coordinates: 35.47°N, 139.62°E

   Coordinates: 45.3°N, 141.05°E
